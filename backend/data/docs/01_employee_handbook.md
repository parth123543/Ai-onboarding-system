# Launch Mate Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## Welcome to the Team
Welcome to Launch Mate! We are thrilled to have you join our mission of empowering every person and organization on the planet to achieve more. This handbook outlines our core workplace policies, values, and practical information you will need during your first weeks.

## Hybrid Workplace & Working Hours
Launch Mate operates under a flexible hybrid model:
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in your local time zone. During these hours, teams are expected to be reachable via Microsoft Teams for standups, syncs, and collaborative sessions.
- **Office Attendance:** Employees in hybrid roles are encouraged to spend 2 to 3 days per week in their local hub office (e.g., Redmond, London, Seattle, Bangalore) to foster in-person mentorship and camaraderie.
- **Quiet Focus Time:** Launch Mate observes 'No-Meeting Fridays' after 1:00 PM local time to encourage uninterrupted deep work and personal development.

## Home Office & Equipment Allowance
To ensure your remote workspace is safe and ergonomic, all full-time new joiners are eligible for:
- **Ergonomic Workspace Stipend:** A one-time reimbursement of up to $1,200 USD (or local currency equivalent) for monitors, ergonomic chairs, motorized standing desks, or keyboards.
- **Expense Claim Window:** Must be submitted via SAP Concur within the first 60 days of employment under the expense category 'Remote Workspace Equipment'.
- **Monthly Internet Subsidy:** $60 USD per month automatically credited on your payroll for high-speed broadband.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policies
- **Casual Leave (CL):** 12 days of paid casual leave per calendar year for personal, urgent, or unforeseen matters. Available immediately upon joining without probation waiting period.
- **Annual Vacation:** 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 days can carry over into the next calendar year.
- **Sick & Caregiver Leave:** 10 days of paid sick leave per year, available immediately upon your start date.
- **Company Holidays:** 12 recognized public and corporate holidays annually.
- **Volunteer Days:** 2 paid days per year dedicated to community service and charitable initiatives.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks of 100% paid leave for all new parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement).

## The Buddy Program
Every new joiner is assigned an Onboarding Buddy from outside their direct reporting line. Your buddy will reach out during Week 1 to set up an informal coffee chat, introduce you to company culture, show you how to navigate internal tools, and answer questions you might feel hesitant to ask your manager.
