# Code of Business Conduct & Ethics

## Our Commitment to Integrity
At Contoso, we build trust through ethical behavior, transparency, and accountability. Every employee, contractor, and intern is expected to embody our standards of professional conduct and mutual respect.

## Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination Policy
- **Zero-Tolerance Standard:** Contoso strictly prohibits harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, veteran status, or any protected legal characteristic.
- **Prohibited Conduct:** Includes unwelcome sexual advances, inappropriate physical contact, offensive slurs or epithets, microaggressions, mocking accents, or creating an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.
- **Reporting Channels:** Any employee experiencing or witnessing misconduct is strongly urged to report it immediately. Reports can be made to:
  - Your People Partner (HR) or direct manager.
  - Confidential Employee Relations team at `conduct@contoso.com`.
  - Anonymous 24/7 Ethics & Whistleblower Hotline: `1-800-ETHIC-MS` or web portal `https://contoso.ethicspoint.com`.
- **Anti-Retaliation Protection:** Contoso strictly forbids any retaliation against an individual who reports a violation in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## Conflicts of Interest & Moonlighting
- Employees must disclose secondary employment, external board seats, advisory roles, or freelance technical consulting through the Compliance Portal (`https://compliance.contoso.com`).
- Moonlighting with competitors, clients, or vendors is strictly prohibited.

## Gifts & Entertainment Policy
- Nominal gifts (e.g. promotional pens, coffee mugs) valued under $50 USD may be accepted from vendors.
- Any gift, meal, or hospitality exceeding $50 USD must be pre-approved by the Compliance Office.
- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, prepaid debit cards) may never be accepted under any circumstances.
