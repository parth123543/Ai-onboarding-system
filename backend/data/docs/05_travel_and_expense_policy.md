# Travel, Entertainment & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## General Expense Guidelines
- **System of Record:** All business expenses must be submitted via SAP Concur (`https://concur.launchmate.com`).
- **Submission Deadline:** Expenses must be submitted with itemized receipts within 30 days of incurring the expense. Late submissions past 60 days may be rejected without reimbursement.
- **Corporate Card:** Full-time employees are issued an American Express Corporate Card for all travel and business meals.

## Air Travel Policy
- **Domestic & Short-Haul Flights (< 6 hours):** Standard Economy / Main Cabin seating. Extra legroom seats (Economy Plus) are reimbursable for flights over 4 hours.
- **Long-Haul & International Flights (> 6 hours continuous):** Business Class / Delta One / Polaris seating is authorized to ensure physical wellbeing and productive arrival.

## Lodging & Hotel Caps
- **Tier 1 Cities (New York, San Francisco, Seattle, London, Tokyo, Zurich):** Maximum reimbursable room rate is $275 USD per night before taxes and mandatory local fees.
- **Tier 2 Cities (Austin, Chicago, Dublin, Singapore, Toronto):** Maximum rate of $200 USD per night.
- **All other locations:** Maximum rate of $160 USD per night.

## Meals & Daily Per Diem
- **Domestic Travel (US/Canada):** Up to $75 USD per full travel day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- **International Travel:** Up to $100 USD per full travel day.
- **Team Dinners:** Reimbursable up to $80 per person including tax and gratuity when celebrating team milestones or welcoming new team members.
